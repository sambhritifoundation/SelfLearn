## Verification rule

A paper was admitted only when its title or internal section heading explicitly identified both:

1. IIT Madras BS in Electronic Systems; and
2. `QUALIFIER LEVEL` or an explicit Electronic Systems Qualifier exam code/title.

Course Quiz 1, Quiz 2, End-Term and Data Science qualifier papers were excluded.
