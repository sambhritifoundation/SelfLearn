EARNING IDEAS

Services people may pay for

Sell a useful finished result and your review, editing and support. AI output by itself is a draft; fees and demand vary by client and location.

01

Social media pack

Create branded posters, captions and short reels for a local shop. Use Canva or Firefly for design, and OpusClip, Runway or Higgsfield for video.

Deliver: approved files, captions and usage notes.

02

Hindi–English learning content

Prepare notes, presentation slides and narrated explainers for tutors or training centres with NotebookLM, Gamma and ElevenLabs.

Deliver: checked notes, slides and consent-cleared audio.

03

Business FAQ and automation

Organise a shop's approved answers and route enquiries using ChatGPT, Zapier, Make or n8n. Keep a human review step.

Deliver: working workflow, FAQ and handover guide.

04

Simple websites and dashboards

Build and test a landing page, booking prototype or data dashboard with Lovable, Replit, Streamlit or other developer tools.

Deliver: usable site, source files and support plan.

Also approach local shops, schools, NGOs and creators with one relevant sample. Check each platform's current eligibility, fees and policies before joining.

Work responsibly

Get permission before uploading private files, using someone's face or cloning a voice. Confirm commercial-use rights for generated assets. Check facts, accessibility and local language quality. Never promise guaranteed income.
