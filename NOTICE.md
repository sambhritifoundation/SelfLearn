### Learner data & privacy

SelfLearn stores learning progress (scores, streaks, attempts) locally in the learner's
own browser. No personal information is collected or transmitted to any server.

### Updating the copyright year

The displayed copyright year is configurable in `index.html` (and its copy
`selflearn-app.html`) via the `SL_CONFIG` object:

```js
var SL_CONFIG = { org: "Sambhriti Foundation for Education", year: 2025, website: "https://sambhriti.in" };
```

Change `year` to update the footer and legal pages across the site.
